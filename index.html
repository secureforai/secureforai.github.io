My academic path

Computer Science, from undergraduate study to doctoral research—grounded in a curiosity about how intelligent systems learn and generalize.

I am Dr. Hidayaturrahman, S.Kom., M.T., a lecturer at the School of Computer Science (SoCS), BINUS University. My academic interests include artificial intelligence, deep learning, domain adaptation, and intelligent systems.

My doctoral research at BINUS University focused on domain expansion in image classification using deep learning and adversarial multitask learning. I’m interested in translating technical concepts into clear explanations, practical experiments, and learning experiences that encourage students to think critically.

This website is a home for my academic interests, selected writing, talks, and professional connections.
